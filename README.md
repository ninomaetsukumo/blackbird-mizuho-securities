## Research boundary / 調査上の境界

This publication does **not** establish a conspiracy, insider-trading scheme, criminal agreement, regulatory violation, or misconduct by any named person or organisation.

本公開資料は、陰謀、インサイダー取引スキーム、犯罪上の合意、規制違反、または特定の個人・組織による不正行為を立証するものではありません。

The public edition deliberately minimises personal identifiers. Individual names are omitted where a role-level description is sufficient to understand the analytical point.

公開版では個人識別情報を意図的に最小化しています。分析上の論点を役職レベルで十分説明できる場合、個人名は省略しています。

## Evidence model / 証拠モデル

Project Blackbird separates:

- **Verified Fact**
- **Reported Information**
- **Analytical Judgment**
- **Hypothesis**
- **Alternative Explanation**
- **Negative Finding**
- **Unknown**

Primary and official sources are preferred where available. A documented association does not imply misconduct, and absence of public evidence is not treated as evidence of concealment.
